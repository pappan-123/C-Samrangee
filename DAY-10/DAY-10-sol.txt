### Important Exam Notes

* `*ptr++` → `*(ptr++)` (use value, then increment pointer)
* `*++ptr` → `*(++ptr)` (increment pointer first)
* `++*ptr` → `++(*ptr)` (increment the value pointed to)
* Array names are **not variables**; they cannot be assigned (`arr++`, `arr = ptr` are illegal).
* `arr[i]` is exactly equivalent to `*(arr + i)` and even `i[arr]`.
* C always uses **pass by value**.
* Returning the address of a local variable results in **undefined behavior**.
* Function arguments are evaluated in an **unspecified order** by the C standard.

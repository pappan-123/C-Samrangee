EXPLANATIONS

1. Non-positional systems do not depend on place value.
2. (10)₁₆ = 1 × 16 + 0 = 16.
3. 2's complement of 0000 remains 0000.
4. A nibble contains 4 bits, so it represents 2⁴ = 16 values.
5. 83₁₀ = 123₈.
6. 5 = 101 and 7 = 111, so 57₈ = 101111₂.
7. In signed binary representation, MSB = 1 means negative.
8. 0011 - 0111 = -4, which is 1100 in 2's complement.
9. (A3)₁₆ = (10 × 16) + 3 = 163.
10. 1's complement means flipping all bits.
11. 0.011₂ = 0/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 = 0.375.
12. Hexadecimal groups binary digits into sets of 4 bits.
13. 1110.11 + 1100.011 = 11011.001.
14. 1101 in 2's complement = -3.
15. G is not a valid hexadecimal digit.
16. 1010 → 0101 + 1 = 0110.
17. Adding 1 to a 1's complement number gives the 2's complement.
18. In hexadecimal, F = 15.
19. 3 bits can represent 2³ = 8 values.
20. Binary to octal conversion requires grouping bits from right to left.
